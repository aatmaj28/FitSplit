### 4. Clone conversation quality
**Why the clones asked for things already said.** All three suspects contributed (details in
`docs/notes/roommate_engine_analysis.md`):
- **How the other clone's message was passed (main cause).**
  - It arrived as an unlabelled "user" message behind the phase hint. Models echoed it (Priya's clone
    repeated Kavya's message word for word for ten turns), answered as the other person, or called
    them "User".
  - Each clone saw only its last 6 history entries. By turn 8 the other's intro (budget, move-in)
    was out of view.
- **Lazy modules.** A clone's own sleep, guests and cleanliness answers arrived only after it asked
  for that module, and only on its next turn. Until then it guessed, often by mirroring the other
  (Meera's clone reported Priya's sleep times as her own).
- **Phase prompts.** "Ask theirs" repeated every turn. "VRD in your final message" put a verdict in
  every message, and a turn-1 "STRONG" once decided a match.
- **Found while regenerating:**
  - the clones and the analysis read "mv=1–3 months" (move in within 1 to 3 months) as a 1–3 month
    stay, and argued about lease length;
  - the analysis invented "hard non-negotiables" and overrode the clones' verdict.

**The fix: engine rm2** (`backend/app/roommates/memory.py`, `engine.py`).
- **Memory.** Every turn's prompt names both clones and labels the other's message ("Meera's clone
  just said: …"). It carries a memory parsed from the ACP fields:
  - what the other already told you (never ask again);
  - what you already shared (don't repeat);
  - what's still open this phase;
  - any fact check from code.
- **Modules by phase.** Exploration loads routines, social and lifestyle; negotiation loads financial
  and dealbreakers. A topic the other raises loads its module before you reply.
- **Intro.** It must state smoking, pets and non-negotiables, so Jake's smoking ends the chat in
  turn 2.
- **Non-negotiables.** Each clone sees its exact non-negotiables and flexible items. Before the
  conclusion, only a conflict with a real non-negotiable ends the chat; anything else gets "negotiate
  it". The same rule applies to an analysis override.
- **Verdicts.** Only conclusion-phase verdicts decide strong / conditional. Code-verified open points
  (e.g. lease length) are listed first.
- **Field notes** for every clone: "mv = when you move in, not how long you stay; your lease: …".
- **Plain-English lines.** Each reply starts with a `SAY:` line, the chat's main text, with the
  protocol under a small per-message **details** toggle. It comes with the turn and is cached with
  it. One cached narration call fills any gaps (none were needed), and narration that quotes a
  number its message doesn't contain is dropped.
- **Tests.** 11 new backend tests. The test fixture now also blocks the text providers and isolates
  the usage ledger and cache (the new narration step would otherwise have let a test reach a real
  API).
